Ahmad Salehiyan is an industrial engineering researcher and doctoral student at Oklahoma State University. His work concerns sequential decision-making under uncertainty, including maintenance planning, warehouse routing, and machine learning for health and industrial data. The university lists him in its doctoral program in Industrial Engineering and Management; his website identifies him as a Ph.D. candidate advised by Akash Deep. [1][2]

## Research

### Maintenance planning

Salehiyan’s maintenance research uses partially observable Markov decision processes, or POMDPs, to represent equipment condition when sensors provide incomplete or noisy information. His project description reports control-limit policies for deciding between continued operation and preventive maintenance, together with belief-state aggregation and state-space compression to address high-dimensional sensor observations. This line of work includes a 2024 TechRxiv preprint with J. Lee, M. Kim, and A. Deep, and a related manuscript listed as submitted to *INFORMS Journal on Data Science*. [3][4]

His dissertation project, *Sequential Decision-Making Under Uncertainty in Smart Engineering Systems*, extends maintenance planning to heterogeneous fleets sharing limited resources. The proposed approach uses decentralized agents and a two-phase safe multi-agent reinforcement-learning design, with soft constraints during exploration and hard constraints during enforcement. The website identifies this as ongoing work and reports no numerical results or operational deployment. [3][5]

### Warehouse routing

With M. Masae, P. Vichitkunakorn, and A. Deep, Salehiyan developed a hierarchical approach to picker routing in two-block warehouses. Dynamic programming handles routing within aisles, while a Double Deep Q-Network selects the aisle sequence. The project page reports total travel-distance reductions of 3.0% to 15.4% against S-shape and largest-gap heuristics in the evaluated settings. The work is listed as an SSRN preprint; the comparison is not a claim of equivalent operating-cost savings or universal performance. [3][6]

### Health data analysis

Salehiyan coauthored *Disease Cluster Analysis in Electronic Health Records: Insights into Mortality and Comorbidity Patterns* with P. Vaghfi Mohebbi and A. Deep. His publication record lists the study in the proceedings of the 2025 IISE Annual Conference & Expo. The project description reports analysis of 179,524 adult patients in MIMIC-IV, using association-rule mining, t-SNE, and k-means clustering to examine disease co-occurrence and mortality patterns. [3][7]

### Industrial cybersecurity

Salehiyan was first author of a 2025 *Future Internet* paper with Pardis Sadatian Moghaddam and Masoud Kaveh. The study combined a transformer, a generative adversarial network, and an autoencoder for intrusion detection, with evaluation on the WUSTL-IIoT-2021, EdgeIIoTset, and TON_IoT datasets. [8]

In 2026, he coauthored a *Computers, Materials & Continua* paper with Nuria Serrano, Francisco Hernando-Gallego, Diego Martín, and José Vicente Álvarez-Bravo. The paper examined a framework combining transformer models, deep reinforcement learning, and evolutionary optimization for detecting false-data-injection attacks in smart water infrastructure. [9]
