# Ahmad Salehiyan: One-Page Profile

Ph.D. Candidate in Industrial Engineering and Management at Oklahoma State University (expected Summer 2027), with a concurrent M.S. in Applied Statistics. Advisor: Dr. Akash Deep.

My research is on reinforcement learning and optimization for decision-making under uncertainty. I work on maintenance planning under partial observability (POMDP models and multi-agent reinforcement learning for fleets), reinforcement learning for warehouse picker routing, and machine learning for health data and industrial security.

I am on the 2026 to 2027 job market for postdoc, faculty, and industry research roles. I am available full time from June 2027 and open to relocation anywhere in the U.S.
